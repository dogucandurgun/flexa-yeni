Glass or solid panel? Choosing façade modules

Façades are made of modules about 112 cm wide, and each side is chosen separately. Deciding between a shop window and a storage wall.

FLEXA’s façade is not one piece. It is made of modules about 112 cm wide, and each side is chosen separately.

How many modules?

Two fit on a 240 side, four on a 480 side and five on a 600 side. The long sides change with the size; the short sides are 240 cm on every size.

Glass

Full-height glass turns the cabin into a shop window. The inside is visible from outside; product, people and light become part of the façade. It is the natural choice for the sides you want to stand out in a busy space.

Solid panel

A solid panel closes off the back. It is used for a storage section, a screen wall, behind shelving or as a large print surface. It is also the simplest way to close a side you don’t want seen.

One side can be an all-glass shop window, the back a solid storage wall.

How to decide

Look at where the cabin will be seen from. The sides visitors approach from can be glass; the sides at the back or used for service can be solid.

In the cabin planner you can change the modules side by side and see the result in 3D.
